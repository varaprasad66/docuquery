NORTHSTAR DEMO HANDBOOK
Synthetic sample data for DocuQuery verification. Not a real company's policy.

1. Leave
Employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days
may carry over. Carried-over days expire on March 31 of the following year.

2. Working arrangements
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval.
Core working hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.

3. Equipment and expenses
The equipment allowance is USD 600 per employee per year. Submit expense claims
within 30 days of purchase, with the original receipt.

4. Learning
The training budget is USD 1,000 per employee per year.

5. Security
Report suspected security incidents within one hour through the internal security
hotline. Access reviews are performed quarterly.

6. Support
Customer support operates Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC.
